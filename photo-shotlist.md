# Photo Shotlist

Status: L2 V5 build-packet candidate.

Photos are evidence and communication aids. They are not fabrication authority
unless paired with measured references and a documented measurement method.

## Reference Capture

| Shot ID | Subject | Purpose | Required Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PH-001 | Existing dundun body, full side view | Compare shell length, rope path, and head seating | Date, instrument voice if known, and whether dimensions were measured separately. |
| PH-002 | Bearing edge closeup | Review edge radius, finish, and hide seating | Include ruler only as context; record measured radius separately. |
| PH-003 | Hoop/ring assembly | Review ring stock, wrap, weld, and rope contact | Record ring diameter and stock size in measurement log. |
| PH-004 | Rope diamonds and verticals | Review lacing path and stretch marker locations | Pair with rope stretch measurements. |
| PH-005 | Cowhide sample before/after soaking | Capture material behavior | Pair with thickness, mass, and area measurements. |
| PH-006 | Bare shell tap-test setup | Document acoustic measurement setup | Record microphone/tuner, room conditions, and file names. |
| PH-007 | Bell/strap mockup | Check ergonomic clearance and snag risk | Record player orientation and interference notes. |

## Authority Rule

Photos remain `reference_only` in `visual-output-register.csv` until a
measurement record or reviewed drawing explicitly controls any dimension shown
in the image.
