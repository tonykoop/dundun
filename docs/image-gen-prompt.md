# Dundun Image-Gen Prompts

Concept visualization only; not fabrication authority. Do not infer shell
diameters, stave widths, rope paths, head dimensions, or tuning intervals from
these prompts.

## Hero Prompt

Photorealistic 3/4 product photograph of a stave-built dundun trio: kenkeni, sangban, and doundounba as cylindrical dual-headed wooden bass drums with natural cowhide heads on both ends, vertical rope tensioning between hoop assemblies, and simple side-bell mounting on at least one drum. Show the three voices in coherent family scale from small/high to large/deep, with warm hardwood staves, practical rope work, and handmade Morgan Drums-inspired construction discipline. Clean studio background, soft directional lighting, realistic scale from a padded stick nearby, 50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic.

## In-Context Prompt

Photorealistic ensemble-workshop scene of the dundun trio staged beside a djembe, one drum being played horizontally with a stick while a small side bell is visible. Include shop measurement context: scanned planning sheet, rope, cowhide, ring assemblies, and a tuner or recorder nearby. Natural light, grounded documentary tone, culturally respectful staging, no invented ceremonial symbols.

## Detail / Macro Prompt

Photorealistic macro close-up of a dundun head and rope system: cowhide stretched over a cylindrical stave-built shell, top and bottom hoop ring assemblies linked by vertical rope, tension diamonds or rope pulls, and wood grain visible between ropes. Show hide texture, rope fibers, ring wrap, and real tension without labels. High-detail macro, shallow depth of field.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, impossible rope paths, fake cultural markings, extra limbs, distorted hands, floating bells, or dimension callouts.
